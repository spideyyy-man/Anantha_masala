# Creative Redesign Architecture — Madurai in Motion: The Living Spice Theatre
**Date:** 24 November 2026  
**Scope:** Full visual, spatial, and interaction redesign preserving the verified Next.js + Drizzle/PostgreSQL commerce, security, and checkout core.

---

## 1. Honest Diagnosis of v1 Failure (Why v1 Scored 4.5 / 10 on Creative Execution)
1. **Boxed Tablecloth Packaging:** Raw pouch photos (`Assets/1.jpg`–`35.jpg`) remained trapped inside rigid rectangular frames showing beige painted wall and patterned brown floral coasters, making every section look like a generic listing grid.
2. **Monotone Beige Canvas:** No chromatic atmosphere transitions between narrative chapters; flat beige cards stacked top-to-bottom like a SaaS starter template.
3. **Weak Opening Scene:** Conventional 2-column split with tiny low-contrast Three.js dots (`165.7 KB gz` wasted on decorative speckles).
4. **Ordinary Category & Product Tiles:** Uniform 4-column card grids with minimal spatial depth, no focus-frame choreography, no scroll-linked ingredient reveal, and no horizontal pantry motion.

## 3. Mapping Approved `GEMINI.md` Effects & Visual Styles Into Concept C
1. **Layered Parallax (Effect #1):** 3-plane hero depth (GLSL shader + Tamil paper-cut watermark + floating real cutout pouch moving on independent scroll/pointer planes).

3. **Scroll-Triggered Ingredient X-Ray Reveal (Effect #8 + X-Ray Style):** Sticky *Ulundhu Kali Maavu* floating pouch paired with sequential reveal of all 8 verified front-label heritage grains (`Assets/22.jpg`).

5. **Continuous Horizontal Product Movement & Sideways Text (Effects #2 & #5):** Continuous Tamil-English ribbon + horizontal floating-plinth Pantry-in-Motion rail with live collection tabs & 3D hover levitation.
6. **Focus-Frame Spotlight Slider (Effect #7):** Interactive 4-flavour Hero mood switcher + Smoked-Clay Madurai Kitchen Focus-Frame slider linked directly to audited SKUs.
